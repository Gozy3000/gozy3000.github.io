Mikasa

Mikasa is a private job-application assistant. It is used only by its owner and is not offered to anyone else.

Privacy policy

Mikasa asks Google for one permission: to create and manage the Google Drive files that Mikasa itself creates (CVs, cover letters and backups). It cannot see any other file in your Drive.

Those files are kept in the owner's own Google Drive and on the owner's private server. Mikasa does not sell or share Google data, does not use it for advertising, and no one else has access to it.

Mikasa's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can remove Mikasa's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
